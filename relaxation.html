Relaxation Tools

A few simple tools to help you slow down, breathe, and reset — no activity list required.

Take a breath

You deserve a moment to pause

Whether you have five minutes or twenty, these tools are designed to help you unwind wherever you are.

Breathing Exercise

4-7-8 Breathing

Inhale for 4 seconds, hold for 7, exhale for 8. Repeat as many times as you like.

Stretching Tips

  • Roll your shoulders slowly, five times in each direction.
  • Gently tilt your head side to side to release neck tension.
  • Stand up and stretch your arms overhead for 10 seconds.
  • Touch your toes slowly to loosen your lower back.

Mindfulness Tips

  • Notice five things you can see around you right now.
  • Focus on the sensation of your feet on the floor.
  • Let thoughts pass without judging them.
  • Take three slow breaths before continuing your day.
