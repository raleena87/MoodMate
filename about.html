About MoodMate

Learn more about our website and how it helps users find activities based on their mood.

What is it?

What is MoodMate?

MoodMate is a simple website that helps users discover activities based on how they are feeling. Users select their:

  • 😊 Mood
  • 📍 Location
  • ⚡ Energy Level

The website then recommends an activity that best matches those choices. Our goal is to encourage healthy habits, relaxation, and enjoyable activities through a simple and user-friendly experience.

Why we built it

Why Was It Created?

This project was created to help people who are unsure what to do when they are feeling bored, stressed, tired, or happy.
